How It Works

Three steps.
One life saved.

01
Open ReRhythm
Launch the app the moment someone collapses near you. One tap sends an emergency alert to everyone within 500 metres and calls the ambulance.
02
Follow AR Guidance
Camera opens. AI overlays real-time guidance directly on the person's chest — showing exactly where to place your hands and tracking compression rate live.

Features

Everything you need.

AR Hand Placement
